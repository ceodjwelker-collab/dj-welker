The problem

Most sellers guess at products and torch their account on claims. The winners on TikTok Shop demonstrate a visible result in five seconds and pay real commission — but picking them is a guess, and one wrong health claim gets your account flagged. PLUG turns product selection into data and keeps content on the right side of the rules.

What it does

01

Trending product data

Real categories, price bands, and commission ranges — the $20–30 impulse band leads, low-commission electronics get flagged.

02

Content studio

Generate hooks and full scripts tuned to what actually demos in a short clip.

03

Compliance checker

Flags medical and health claims and risky language before you post, so you protect your account.

04

Winner scoring

Demand-to-effort plus commission scoring, so you promote first what pays best.

05

Starter product packs

Ready-to-use product lists so you start with proven picks, not a blank page.

06

Your first move

Opinionated "do this today" guidance instead of a wall of options.
