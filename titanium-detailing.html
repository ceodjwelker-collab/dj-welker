How I did it

The constraint was speed: a working, bookable business inside a week, not a three-month brand project. So I sequenced it the way it actually needs to convert — identity first so the business looks legitimate, then a mobile-first site because nearly every detailing lead comes from a phone, then the local pages and Google Business profile so the business is findable in the specific towns it serves.

The piece most people skip is the loop after the job. I wired an automatic review engine so finished work turns into fresh five-star reviews without the owner chasing anyone — which is what compounds local ranking over time. Everything was handed over client-owned, with a private dashboard to run bookings, leads, and reviews, so there's no dependency on me to keep it running.
